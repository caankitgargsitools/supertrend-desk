Strategy type

Strategy
Supertrend

Breakout: after a buy signal the order triggers only if the next candle trades above the signal candle's high + buffer (sell: below its low − buffer). If it doesn't, the order is cancelled and re-placed from that candle's high / low, and so on until it fills or the signal turns. The trade is filled at the trigger price on normal candles.

Conditions

Conditions are checked each time a candle of the decision timeframe above closes (or, once-a-day, at the session open on yesterday's daily candle), using the latest completed candle of each condition's own timeframe. Orders go in at that close. A long is closed by the “Exit long” rules if you add any; otherwise by the Sell rules (reversing into a short); with neither, as soon as the Buy rules stop being true. Shorts mirror this.

Session (IST)

Stop and reverse: when Supertrend turns green the strategy buys (closing any short first); when it turns red it sells the long and goes short. Decisions are taken when a candle closes. Nothing is traded at or after “No trades after”. If Supertrend flips after that, the next day either waits for the first candle to close and follows Supertrend as it stands then (no trade if it has turned back), or reverses straight away at “First trade from”.

Order format

One order leg in Dhan's webhook JSON. Placeholders: {side} {qty} {exchange} {symbol} {product} {sort} and, for options, {option_type} {strike} {expiry}. Compare with the sample JSON Dhan generates on its Webhooks page and edit the field names here if they differ.

Brokerage & statutory charges (rates in %)

Worked out per trade the way the broker's calculator does: brokerage on each order (futures: the lower of the ₹ amount above and the % below; options: flat ₹ per order), STT on the sell side, exchange and SEBI charges on total turnover, GST on brokerage + exchange + SEBI, stamp duty on the buy side. STT and stamp duty are rounded to the rupee, as the exchanges do. Net P&L per trade is then taken in whole rupees.

Research

Strategy Lab

The lab runs once a day at 16:05 IST or non-stop (see Lab settings). Each run invents new condition strategies (Supertrend, RSI, EMA/SMA, MACD, VWAP, Bollinger, ADX, previous-day and opening-range levels, many with a stop loss, target or trailing stop), backtests each one on the past year with charges, and re-tests the best of the last 30 days on the rolling year. About 40% of each night goes to the winners: each is tried on the other assets, on other decision timeframes, with other stop loss and target settings, with trailing stops to lock in profit, with other indicator combinations, and with small steps on every indicator setting (lengths ±1, ±2 and ±10%, Supertrend factor and Bollinger width ±0.25/±0.5, RSI / ADX levels ±2/±5), which get a double share. About a quarter of new strategies are built around liquidity sweeps. Strategies that keep passing night after night get the most tries, so the strongest ideas are tested hardest. You can also run variations of any one strategy yourself (“Run variations”). The best index strategies are then priced with real expired-option prices, as option buying and option writing. Strategies are ranked on the first 9 months and must also make money in the last 3 months, which the ranking never saw (round 1); then work on other assets and other timeframes (round 2); then make money in every one of the past 10 years (5 years for 5-minute strategies, the history Dhan keeps), so nothing passes on one big move of the underlying (round 3). Strategies that fail are cleared straight away; a one-line record stays so the lab never tries them again.

Assets to research

Indices are tested on 5-minute candles (and once-a-day); commodities on daily candles, with MCX 5-minute candles (from the contracts Dhan still lists, growing every night) used for the timeframe test. Mini contracts move like their big contracts, so testing both mostly repeats work. In non-stop mode, back-to-back runs reuse candles fetched in the last 6 hours, and a run stops by itself if a strategy goes live outside 11:30 PM – 8:30 AM.

Results as

Score = net profit of the first 9 months ÷ the worst drawdown in them × the share of those months that made money. Figures are for 1 lot after brokerage and statutory charges, in whole rupees. Avg margin / lot = for each trade, the margin one lot needed at that trade's entry price (futures: price × lot × Dhan's margin rate; option writing: strike × lot × Dhan's writing margin rate; option buying: premium paid × lot), averaged over the trades. Margin rates come from Dhan's margin calculator, checked each night. Return on margin = the year's net ÷ that average. Similar strategies (same asset, same indicators and conditions, only settings such as lengths, levels, timeframe, stops or session differ) are grouped into one line showing the best of them; open “similar” to see the rest. Nights = how many runs tested this exact strategy (it passed in how many).
Passed needs two rounds. Round 1: profitable in the ranked 9 months and in the 3 months after. Round 2 (the tougher test, on the latest year): the same rules must work on at least 2 other assets (same family first) and on other candle timeframes (at least 2 of them, or 1 when fewer than 3 could be tried; a strategy that can't be tried on another timeframe doesn't pass; once-a-day strategies are tried at the close of 60- and 125-minute candles. MCX 5-minute candles come from the contracts Dhan still lists, so they go back as far as the current contracts have traded and grow every night). Buy & hold (1 lot held for the year) is shown alongside for comparison but isn't part of the pass, because strategies trade short as well as long. “Works” = makes money with a profit factor of 1.1 or more and enough trades. Every earlier result has been put through round 2 as well.
