Strategy
Supertrend

Breakout: after a buy signal the order triggers only if the next candle trades above the signal candle's high + buffer (sell: below its low − buffer). If it doesn't, the order is cancelled and re-placed from that candle's high / low, and so on until it fills or the signal turns. The trade is filled at the trigger price on normal candles.

Session (IST)

Stop and reverse: when Supertrend turns green the strategy buys (closing any short first); when it turns red it sells the long and goes short. Decisions are taken when a candle closes. Nothing is traded at or after “No trades after”. If Supertrend flips after that, the next day either waits for the first candle to close and follows Supertrend as it stands then (no trade if it has turned back), or reverses straight away at “First trade from”.

Order format (advanced)

One order leg in Dhan's webhook JSON. Placeholders: {side} {qty} {exchange} {symbol} {product} {sort} and, for options, {option_type} {strike} {expiry}. Compare with the sample JSON Dhan generates on its Webhooks page and edit the field names here if they differ.

Quick period:
Brokerage & statutory charges (rates in %)

Worked out per trade the way the broker's calculator does: brokerage on each order (futures: the lower of the ₹ amount above and the % below; options: flat ₹ per order), STT on the sell side, exchange and SEBI charges on total turnover, GST on brokerage + exchange + SEBI, stamp duty on the buy side. STT and stamp duty are rounded to the rupee, as the exchanges do. Net P&L per trade is then taken in whole rupees.

Uses the strategy's saved settings. Option trades are priced from Dhan's expired-options data (5-minute, up to 5 years back, strikes within 10 of ATM). Futures are approximated with index prices. Up to 5 years per run; long runs are priced in batches and take a few minutes (roughly 1 minute per year). You can leave the page and come back to the result.
