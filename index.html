Strategy type

Strategy
Supertrend

Breakout: after a buy signal the order triggers only if the next candle trades above the signal candle's high + buffer (sell: below its low − buffer). If it doesn't, the order is cancelled and re-placed from that candle's high / low, and so on until it fills or the signal turns. The trade is filled at the trigger price on normal candles.

Conditions

Conditions are checked each time a candle of the decision timeframe above closes (or, once-a-day, at the session open on yesterday's daily candle), using the latest completed candle of each condition's own timeframe. Orders go in at that close. A long is closed by the “Exit long” rules if you add any; otherwise by the Sell rules (reversing into a short); with neither, as soon as the Buy rules stop being true. Shorts mirror this.

Session (IST)

Stop and reverse: when Supertrend turns green the strategy buys (closing any short first); when it turns red it sells the long and goes short. Decisions are taken when a candle closes. Nothing is traded at or after “No trades after”. If Supertrend flips after that, the next day either waits for the first candle to close and follows Supertrend as it stands then (no trade if it has turned back), or reverses straight away at “First trade from”.

Order format

One order leg in Dhan's webhook JSON. Placeholders: {side} {qty} {exchange} {symbol} {product} {sort} and, for options, {option_type} {strike} {expiry}. Compare with the sample JSON Dhan generates on its Webhooks page and edit the field names here if they differ.

Brokerage & statutory charges (rates in %)

Worked out per trade the way the broker's calculator does: brokerage on each order (futures: the lower of the ₹ amount above and the % below; options: flat ₹ per order), STT on the sell side, exchange and SEBI charges on total turnover, GST on brokerage + exchange + SEBI, stamp duty on the buy side. STT and stamp duty are rounded to the rupee, as the exchanges do. Net P&L per trade is then taken in whole rupees.

Research

Strategy Lab

Assets to research in depth

A run on only the assets you tick: every strategy in it is new (no re-tests of earlier winners, no variations), shared between those assets, and goes through the same three rounds. It replaces the automatic run that is going on now; the automatic runs carry on after it.

How the lab worksruns, rounds, costs, crypto

The lab runs once a day at 16:05 IST or non-stop (see Lab settings). Each run invents new condition strategies (Supertrend, RSI, EMA/SMA, MACD, VWAP, Bollinger, ADX, previous-day and opening-range levels, many with a stop loss, target or trailing stop), backtests each one on the past year with charges, and re-tests the best of the last 30 days on the rolling year (once a day, and only at night, 11:30 PM – 8:30 AM). A run stopped by hand pauses the lab for an hour, then it carries on by itself. Nothing is left half-way: strategies a stopped or failed run hadn’t backtested yet, or that were waiting for option prices, are taken over by the next run first (they count towards its size), and earlier winners still in the tougher test carry on in every run until they pass or fail. “Focused run” researches only the assets you pick, with new strategies only. About 40% of each night goes to the winners: each is tried on the other assets, on other decision timeframes, with other stop loss and target settings, with trailing stops to lock in profit, with other indicator combinations, and with small steps on every indicator setting (lengths ±1, ±2 and ±10%, Supertrend factor and Bollinger width ±0.25/±0.5, RSI / ADX levels ±2/±5), which get a double share. About a quarter of new strategies are built around liquidity sweeps. Strategies that keep passing night after night get the most tries, so the strongest ideas are tested hardest. You can also run variations of any one strategy yourself (“Run variations”). Every index strategy that passes (on intraday candles) then joins the option-pricing queue: in the background, one strategy at a time and oldest first, it is priced with real expired-option prices as option buying, option writing and spreads, only while no run is going. After each run the lab rests about as long as the run worked (10–45 minutes), and whenever the database answers slowly it gives way for 20 minutes, so logins and live trading always come first. Strategies are ranked on the first 9 months and must also make money in the last 3 months, which the ranking never saw (round 1); then, under the simple rules (since 27 Oct 2026), make money in all but one year in five of the past 10 years (8 of 10; 4 of 5 for 5-minute strategies, the history Dhan keeps; 3 of the last 4 quarters where there is under 3 years of minute prices), stay profitable in total with the slippage doubled, have enough trades (30 for daily, 100 for minute strategies) and make money in the held-back latest months that no test saw. Strategies are then ranked by the consistency score: steady profit year after year and quarter after quarter scores more than the same profit made in a few good stretches. Luck, the best trades left out and similar figures are shown for information; passing means “worth paper trading”, not “proven”. Every backtest fills each order a little worse than the test price (slippage set per asset from how liquid it is: 0.01% on Nifty and Gold up to 0.04–0.05% on Midcap Select, Nifty Next 50, Bankex, Nickel and the mini contracts; 0.5–2% of premium on options). Variations come from at most 6 winners per asset, so no single asset fills the runs. Crypto (Bitcoin, Ether, Solana, XRP and any coin added under Connections) is tested as Delta Exchange perpetual futures round the clock, every day, on daily, weekly and 15-minute-based candles (Binance spot history before the coin traded properly on Delta India), with Delta's 0.05% fee + GST, Delta's actual funding (longs pay, shorts receive) and per-coin slippage. Crypto strategies are mostly slow trend followers that trade both ways: Supertrend and Heikin Ashi on daily and weekly candles, moving averages, Donchian breakouts, dips bought only in an uptrend, the late-US-evening hours, with volatility and Bitcoin-trend filters. Strategies that fail are cleared straight away; a one-line record stays so the lab never tries them again.

Assets to research

Indices are tested on 5-minute candles (and once-a-day); commodities on daily candles, with MCX 5-minute candles (from the contracts Dhan still lists, growing every night) used for the timeframe test. Mini contracts move like their big contracts, so testing both mostly repeats work. In non-stop mode, back-to-back runs reuse candles fetched in the last 6 hours, and while any strategy is active (paper or live) the lab, including option pricing, works only 11:30 PM – 8:30 AM; a run going outside that window stops by itself. Runs you start yourself (“Run now”, variations, manual tests) still run when you ask.

What the columns meanscore, margin, passed

Score = net profit of the first 9 months ÷ the worst drawdown in them × the share of those months that made money. Figures are for 1 lot after brokerage and statutory charges, in whole rupees. Avg margin / lot = for each trade, the margin one lot needed at that trade's entry price (futures: price × lot × Dhan's margin rate; option writing: strike × lot × Dhan's writing margin rate; option buying: premium paid × lot), averaged over the trades. Margin rates come from Dhan's margin calculator, checked each night. Return on margin = the year's net ÷ that average. Similar strategies (same asset, same indicators and conditions, only settings such as lengths, levels, timeframe, stops or session differ) are grouped into one line showing the best of them; open “similar” to see the rest. Nights = how many runs tested this exact strategy (it passed in how many).
Passed needs two rounds. Round 1: profitable in the ranked 9 months and in the 3 months after. Round 2 (the tougher test, on the latest year): the same rules must work on at least 1 other asset (same family first) and on other candle timeframes (at least 2 of them, or 1 when fewer than 3 could be tried; a strategy that can't be tried on another timeframe doesn't pass; once-a-day strategies are tried at the close of 60- and 125-minute candles. MCX 5-minute candles come from the contracts Dhan still lists, so they go back as far as the current contracts have traded and grow every night). Buy & hold (1 lot held for the year) is shown alongside for comparison but isn't part of the pass, because strategies trade short as well as long. “Works” = makes money with a profit factor of 1.1 or more and enough trades. Every earlier result has been put through round 2 as well.

    Strategy Lab

    Combinations

    Pairs and threes of strategies that passed the lab, on the same asset and the same timeframe, built on different rules (twins and settings-only variants count as the same rules, so the best of each is used). For each combination: how often they sit on opposite sides while two or more are in a trade, each one's profit and biggest fall, and the same together. A combination lessens the drawdown when they are on opposite sides at least 20% of the time they are in a trade together and profit ÷ biggest fall together is better than every member alone. Figures from the lab's backtests (futures, 1 lot each, after charges), over the years all members were tested. In one Dhan account, opposite positions in the same contract net off: how that works in practice is still to be checked.

    Research

    Prediction Lab

    Chaturbhuj, our AI model trained on candlestick charts (built on an open-source model), forecasts every lab asset on 5-minute, 15-minute, 30-minute, 1-hour, 4-hour and daily candles, 1 to 10 candles ahead. Each forecast is checked against what actually happened and against three simple guesses it has to beat: the price stays the same (closing price), the last move continues (direction) and the recent average swing (high–low range). The replay covers only the months after the base model's training data (from September 2025), so it is tested on prices it has never seen; live forecasts are counted separately. It runs on the AWS server and only public market prices go there. Watch only: nothing here places a trade. Next steps, each with your go-ahead: paper trading, then live.
