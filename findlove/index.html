Lost and found children · scaffold
If a child is missing or found right now, call first: Childline 1098 (free, 24x7) · Police 112 · then file an FIR at the nearest police station and tell the district Child Welfare Committee. Find Love helps you record and match; it never replaces these.

What Find Love does

A parent reports a lost child; a volunteer, shelter or police post reports a found child. The system puts likely pairs side by side by age, sex, place and time. A verified person checks each pair with the family and the authorities before anything is called a match.
